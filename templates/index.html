How to read this

BUY: the Supertrend turned bullish within the last few days and all 5 filters pass (ADX ≥ 22 with +DI above −DI, choppiness ≤ 55, price above the 200 EMA, RSI above 50, volume above its 20-day average). NEAR: a recent flip with 4 of 5 filters passing; "Missing" shows which one blocks it, so watch it and do not buy yet.

Stop is the Supertrend line, Target is 2× the risk, and Qty sizes the position so a stop-out costs your chosen risk % of capital (NEAR shows a half-size starter). Stocks with under a year of history are skipped. Check results, upcoming events and the price band (circuit limit) before any paper trade.
